# MeetGill Med (preview)

**Open the live preview: https://mgxll23.github.io/meetgill-med/**

Early preview of MeetGill Med: virtual training instruments for nursing and medical students. The first instrument is a practice infusion pump simulator.

**Unofficial practice tool.** Not clinical reference and not affiliated with any equipment manufacturer. Drug names, concentrations and limits are illustrative only. Always follow your facility's policy and the manufacturer's Instructions for Use.

This is a draft: prices, sign-in and checkout screens are placeholders and nothing on them is connected.
