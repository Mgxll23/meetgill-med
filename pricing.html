Questions

Good to know.

What happens after the 3 days?

Your trial ends and the locked features stay locked. You can pick a plan whenever you are ready.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Account and billing controls will be added when payments are connected.

Is this clinical reference?

No. It is an unofficial practice simulator for learning sequences. Drug names, concentrations and limits are illustrative only. Always follow your facility's policy and the manufacturer's Instructions for Use.

What devices does it work on?

It runs in a modern browser on a laptop or tablet. Nothing to install.

I have a billing question.

Write to billing@meetgillmed.com and tell us which plan you chose. Payments will be handled by our payment provider.

Which instruments are included?

The infusion pump is live today. More instruments are planned and will be included with paid plans as they are released.
