About

Practice should not need a patient.

MeetGill Med builds virtual training instruments so nursing and medical students can practise on realistic equipment before they use the real thing.

Our story

It started in a nursing lab.

A nursing student had an IV return demo coming up and could not get enough time on the real pump. So we built a simulator around the lab's own workflow: load the line, prime, program, start.

Classmates tried it and wanted it too. That is how MeetGill Med began, and why it is built around what students are actually marked on.

The infusion pump is the first instrument. Others are planned, one at a time, each built the same way: from real lab practice and real workflows.

What we believe

Three rules we build by.

01

Mistakes are cheap here

A wrong key on a simulator costs nothing. Make the mistake, see exactly which step it was, and fix it before it counts.

02

Built from real labs

We design around real lab workflows and what instructors mark, not around what looks impressive in a demo.

03

Honest about limits

This is practice, not clinical reference. We say so clearly, and we do not invent proof, users or claims.
