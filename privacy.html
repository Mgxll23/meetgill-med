Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

Summary

We collect as little as we can. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. This is a draft and will be updated as accounts and payments are added.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us. Your name, email address and message when you contact us.
  • Account details. When accounts launch: your email address and the plan you choose.
  • Payments. Card payments are handled by our payment provider on its own secure page. We do not see or store your card number.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.

At the time of writing this site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

To reply to you, to provide and secure the service, to process payments through our provider, and to improve the product. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the site, such as hosting, email and payment processing, and only as needed to provide those services. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required for accounting and legal reasons.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to privacy@meetgillmed.com.

Children

The service is meant for students of nursing and medicine and is not directed at young children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@meetgillmed.com.
