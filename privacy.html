Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Summary

MeetGill Med is a free practice website for nursing and medical students, run by Meet Gill in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. We collect as little as we can. There are no accounts, no payments, no advertising and no tracking. This page explains what we collect, why, where it is kept and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Meet Gill is responsible for privacy at MeetGill Med. Reach the privacy contact at meetgillmed@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us. The contact form opens your own email app. When you send the email, we receive your name, email address and message.
  • Reviews. Your name (we keep only your first name and last initial), your program if you add it, your ratings, your comments and whether you would use the pump. Approved reviews appear on the Reviews page and the home page.
  • Simulator requests and votes. The simulator you suggest, any details you add and your name if you give one (first name and last initial only). Requests appear on the Contact page straight away. To allow one vote per person, your browser creates a random voting code. We store only a scrambled version of it, never your name.
  • Change requests. Which simulator it is about, your message and your name if you give one. Only we see these.
  • Spam protection. To limit abuse of the forms, your IP address is turned into a scrambled code that cannot be reversed. It is deleted automatically after about an hour. We do not store IP addresses.
  • Basic technical data. The companies that host the site (GitHub and Cloudflare) process your IP address and browser details to deliver pages, and may keep standard server logs for security.

Please do not include health information about real patients, or anyone else's personal details, in anything you send us.

Cookies and browser storage

This site does not use cookies, advertising or analytics. It saves a few small items in your browser's own storage so the site works the way you left it. These stay on your device and are not sent to us:

  • your light or dark mode choice;
  • your random voting code and the requests you have voted for;
  • the pump's last settings (care unit, profile, last infusion, volume totals and battery level), so it picks up where you left off;
  • scenarios you build with "Make your own scenario" in the pump, or add from a share link;
  • your practice scores, your Coach or Exam choice, and whether you have seen the pump tour.

You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

How we use it

To reply to you, to show reviews and requests, to decide what to build next, to keep the site secure and to improve the product. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers and where your data is stored

We use a few service providers to run the site: GitHub (hosting the website), Cloudflare (hosting the website and storing reviews, requests and change requests) and Google (the Gmail inbox that receives your emails). These companies are based in the United States and may store or process information outside Canada. Information held outside Canada is subject to the laws of those countries and may be accessed by their courts and authorities. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Reviews and requests: while they are shown on the site, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Change requests and emails: as long as we need them to look into and answer your message.
  • Spam-protection codes: deleted automatically after about an hour.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, including taking down a review or request, by writing to meetgillmed@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards. Submissions are sent over encrypted connections, the review admin page is password protected, and we keep only what we need. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive information.

Age

The site is meant for college and university students. If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us a review, request or message.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, for example when accounts or payments are added, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email meetgillmed@gmail.com.
